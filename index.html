How it works

From blocked in to driving off in 60 seconds.

Three steps. No tow trucks. No yelling. No waiting around.

  1. 1

    Register your car

    Sign in with your phone number, then snap your plate and car licence so we know the car is yours.

  2. 2

    Search the plate

  3. 3

    Send an alert

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can see my phone number or name?

Nobody. Searching a plate only tells someone that the car is registered. They can send you an alert, and you can reply, but neither of you ever learns who the other is. If someone misuses alerts, you can block or report them from the alert itself.

What happens when someone alerts my car?

You get a notification saying what's wrong: it's blocking someone, the lights are on, the alarm is going off. Reply “On my way”, “5 minutes” or “My car isn't there” with one tap, and they are told straight away.

What if the car blocking me isn't registered?

The app tells you the car isn't on Tal3ni yet. Register your own car so the next person who's stuck can alert you, and tell your friends. Every new driver makes Tal3ni more useful.

Why do I photograph my car licence?

To prove the car is really yours, so nobody can register someone else's plate. We read the plate from both photos and check they match. The photos are then discarded; they're never stored.

How do I sign in?

With your Egyptian phone number. We send you a 6-digit code on WhatsApp, with no password to remember.

Is it free? How many cars can I add?

Tal3ni is free to use. You can register up to two cars on one account.
