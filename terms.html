2. Description of Service

Tal3ni lets users:

  • Register a vehicle by proving they own it
  • Search for a registered vehicle by its plate number
  • Contact the owner of a registered vehicle by phone

The service exists so that a driver who is blocked in, or who needs to alert an owner about their car, can reach that owner. It is not a directory, a lookup service for hire, or a source of data for any other purpose.

3. Vehicle Verification

You cannot register a plate by typing it. To register a vehicle you must photograph its number plate and its vehicle licence, and the plate number read from both must match.

  • You may only register vehicles that you own or are lawfully entitled to register
  • Registering a vehicle that is not yours is a violation of these Terms and grounds for termination
  • A verified plate cannot be edited afterwards. If a plate is registered incorrectly, delete the vehicle and register it again
